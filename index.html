04 — About

I, Greg Montez, am a singer, songwriter, and producer aspiring to bring my life story to listeners around the world.

I’m inspired by many! From God through Christianity to Ariana Grande, Eminem, and other artists, I love blending many different inspirations in my life to create music.

My purpose in writing is to tell pieces of my story while leaving room for my listeners to hopefully relate to it and find their own meaning within it.

I’m still discovering my sound, who I am, and where this journey will take me. But it starts with this album and studying at Berklee College of Music! I hope you will join me on this journey into a world of music — a world that’s completely and unapologetically mine.
