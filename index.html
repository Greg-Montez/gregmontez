03 — About

I, Greg Montez, am a singer, songwriter, and producer, drawn to music where sound meets emotion and storytelling.

I'm inspired by many artists, such as Ariana Grande, and I love blending the world of pop with honest writing and different sounds.

For me, my purpose in making music is about telling pieces of my story while leaving room for my listeners to relate to it and find their own meaning within it.

I'm still discovering my sound, who I am, and where this journey will take me. I would love for you to join me on this journey into a world created through my music — a world that feels completely and unapologetically mine.
